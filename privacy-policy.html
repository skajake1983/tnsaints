[[OWNER: exact legal name, e.g. "Tennessee Saints Basketball Academy LLC" — plan item O12]] ("Tennessee Saints," "we," "us") runs the website tnsaints.com, the parent portal at portal.tnsaints.com, and our registration and payment services. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Most of what we hold is about children, and we treat it that way.

1. What we collect

When you contact us or show interest

The forms on our website ask for your name, email and phone number and, depending on the form, your child's name, grade, school and position, your coaching background, your business or organisation, and your message.

When you register for an evaluation

Your child's name, grade, school and years of experience; your name, email and phone; an emergency contact; any medical or allergy notes you choose to share; your acceptance of the waiver with your typed signature; and whether we may use photos of your child.

When you use the parent portal

  • Your account: your email address, and how you sign in — by email link, or with Google if you choose (we then receive your name and email address from Google, never your password).
  • Your family: guardians' names, phone numbers and relationship to the children; emergency contacts.
  • Your children: name, date of birth, grade, school and shirt size; your answer to our medical question (either "nothing to declare" or the details you give).
  • Programs: applications, the group and schedule of any place we offer, signed waivers (with the waiver's exact text, your typed signature and the time), and your photo choice.
  • Payments: the amount, date and PayPal reference of each payment. We never see or store card or bank details — PayPal handles those.
  • Devices: for each device you are signed in on, the browser type and when it was last used, so you can sign other devices out.

What our staff record

  • Evaluations: coaches' feedback on how your child played. Some of it is written for you and sent to you; some is internal to our coaches and is not shared.
  • Our notes: when we call, email or meet you about an inquiry or your family's place, we may note what was discussed so the next person who helps you knows the history. We never record medical details in these notes.
  • Team schedules: practices and games for teams. Calendar links we give families contain the schedule only — no children's names.
  • Donations (once we are a recognised non-profit): a donor's name, address and the gift, so we can send the acknowledgment the IRS asks for.

Collected automatically

  • Google Analytics on our public website: pages visited, how you arrived, browser and device, and approximate (city-level) location, using cookies. It does not receive your name or email. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Security: to stop automated abuse we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your connection's IP address with each form submission for 30 days, and use Cloudflare Turnstile to check a real person is filling in our forms.
  • Sign-in cookies: the parent portal uses cookies that keep you signed in. They are needed for the portal to work and are not used for advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and arrange evaluations.
  • To run our programs: review applications, offer places in scheduled groups, take payment, and keep rosters, schedules and emergency information ready for practices and games.
  • To keep children safe: emergency contacts and medical answers are there so we can act quickly if something happens.
  • To send you what you need about your child's place — offers, confirmations, schedules and evaluation feedback. We do not send marketing to anyone who has not asked to hear from us.
  • To keep our own records as a business [[OWNER: and, once recognised, a non-profit]] — payments, waivers and, where required, donations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

3. Who helps us handle it

These services process information for us, only to provide their service:

  • Cloudflare — runs the parent portal, our registration service and its database, checks for bots (Turnstile), and protects staff sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the public website.
  • PayPal — takes payments. PayPal receives the program's name and an internal reference number, never your child's name.
  • Resend — delivers the emails our system sends (sign-in links, offers, confirmations).
  • Google — website analytics, and sign-in if you choose "Sign in with Google."
  • Formspree — a backup for our website forms, used only if our own system is briefly unavailable.
  • Microsoft 365 — our staff email, and board documents. [[OWNER: confirm]]
  • Font Awesome (via cdnjs) — icons on the website.

4. Children's information

Information about children is given to us by their parents or guardians. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13. Inside our systems:

  • Medical answers can be read only by academy administrators, one child at a time, and every read is recorded. Coaches see only that a note exists, so they know to ask.
  • Coaches see what they need to coach — names, grades and shirt sizes — not families' contact details.
  • Board members, who oversee the organisation, cannot see any child's information.
  • Everyone who works with players completes a background check and safety training before they start.

5. How long we keep it

[[OWNER / ATTORNEY: confirm these periods before publishing — plan item O15]]

  • A child's medical answer: removed 90 days after their last place with us ends.
  • Medical notes given for an evaluation: removed 90 days after the evaluation.
  • Inquiries from people who never joined: anonymised after 24 months without contact.
  • Signed waivers: kept for 7 years, and at least until the child turns 21.
  • Payment records: kept for 7 years.
  • Scrambled IP addresses from forms: 30 days. Email sign-in links expire after 15 minutes; signed-in sessions after at most 30 days.

If a dispute, claim or safety concern requires it, we may keep specific records longer, until it is resolved.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: your family's details, children and medical answers are on your family page in the portal, where you can update them.
  • Download: from your portal Account page, download everything we hold about your family, in a single file.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your family's information from your portal Account page, or by email. We will confirm with you and tell you when it is done. We keep signed waivers and payment records for the periods above, as the law and our insurers require.
  • Photos: you choose whether we may use photos of your child, and you can change your answer at any time.
  • Emails: tell us if you no longer want to hear from us about anything but your child's current place.
  • Analytics and cookies: you can block cookies or use the Google opt-out above. Portal sign-in needs its own cookies.

7. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Staff sign in through Cloudflare Access and see only what their role needs. Passwords are not used anywhere; sign-in links and sessions are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Access to medical information is logged. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what you trust us with and will tell you promptly if a problem affects your information.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new date, and, for changes that matter to families with a portal account, tell you by email.
