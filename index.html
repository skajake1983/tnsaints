Tennessee Saints is a year-round Christian youth basketball academy in Middle Tennessee. We believe faith, culture, and community are the foundation of great players and great coaches. We develop players in game-like situations instead of standing in lines running drills, so athletes learn to read the floor, make quick decisions, and play with confidence. Players train weekly for a minimum of three hours with regular scrimmages, plus optional tournament and league play. Our hope is that every player falls deeper in love with the game and enjoys the journey — growing physically, mentally, and spiritually along the way.

Mission

Our Mission

Tennessee Saints exists to develop young athletes through faith, discipline, and evidence-informed training. Our mission is to grow complete players and people through intentional coaching, weekly competitive reps, and a culture that values character, leadership, and team-first basketball.

Values

Our Core Values

Team over self

Basketball is a team game. We teach players to communicate, trust teammates, and compete together with discipline and effort.

Character over stats

We believe strong character starts with strong values. Our program emphasizes humility, gratitude, and respect both on and off the court.

Christ over everything

Every player should improve through faith. We develop the whole athlete — grounded in purpose, guided by Christ, and growing year after year.

Program

Who We Train

Our current groups are 4th through 6th graders, training weekly year-round with regular scrimmages plus optional tournament and league play. We are actively exploring additional grade levels — so if your player falls outside that range, reach out anyway. Which groups we add next depends on the interest we hear from families, and we would much rather know you are out there than have you count yourself out.

Development Focus
  • Ball handling, passing, and shooting fundamentals
  • Defensive effort, positioning, and communication
  • Basketball IQ and decision making
  • Team-first mentality and leadership
  • Weekly academy training (minimum 3 hours) plus regular scrimmages
  • Optional tournament and league play

Our Coaches & Leadership

Our PGC partnership gives our families discounted rates on PGC camps, and Transforming Basketball supports our coach education and training.
